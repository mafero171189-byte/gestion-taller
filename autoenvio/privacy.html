AutoEnvio Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 4, 2026

1. Summary

AutoEnvio has no user accounts. Your messages, contacts, recipients, rules and PIN stay on this phone: the app does not send them to any server. The app uses the Internet to download its configuration (for example, the free-version limits) and, only if you use the email feature, to send your emails through Gmail. If you choose to send us a diagnostic report, it goes out through your own email app (see section 4). The app has no ads. We do not sell your data or share it with anyone.

2. Who is responsible and how to contact us

The app is published by Mi Taller Web, which is responsible for this policy. Contact: mafero171189@gmail.com.

3. Third-party services

  • Firebase Remote Config (Google): downloads the app's configuration. See section 5.
  • Google Play (billing): processes the payment for AutoEnvio Pro. See section 11.
  • Gmail (Google), only if you use the email feature: sends your emails with your account.
  • SMS, WhatsApp, WhatsApp Business, Telegram, Messenger and Instagram: the apps and services your messages are sent through. Each one is governed by its own policy (see section 10).

The app does not include advertising, analytics or user-profiling SDKs.

4. Diagnostics and error reports

The app keeps a short diagnostic log on your phone. It only records which screen and which buttons the app found, with no names or chat text. This log never leaves the phone on its own.

If something fails, you can tap Send in Settings → Diagnostics. Your email app opens (Gmail, if installed) with a message addressed to mafero171189@gmail.com containing the app version, the phone model, the Android version and the latest log events. You can read and edit it before sending, and it is only sent if you send it from your email app. We use that report only to find and fix the error. If you want us to delete a report you sent us, write to that address.

5. Downloaded configuration

So that we can adjust the free-version limits without you having to update the app, AutoEnvio connects to Firebase Remote Config (a Google service). In doing so, that service may receive normal technical connection data: an identifier of the app installation, the phone model and Android version, the app version and the IP address. No message, contact, recipient or other personal data of yours is sent. Google handles that data under its own privacy policy and may process it on servers in several countries. If there is no Internet connection, the app works normally with the last saved values.

6. What the app stores on your phone

  • Your scheduled sends: recipients (number and name), messages, dates and attachments (a copy in the app's private folder).
  • The send history and its errors.
  • Your auto-reply and forwarding rules, and your templates.
  • Your preferences.
  • If you turn it on, your PIN: it is stored encrypted on your phone (with a key from the Android secure keystore, AES-256), never leaves it, and is used only to unlock the screen at the moment a scheduled message is sent. It is optional: you can turn it off at any time in Settings, and turning it off deletes it.
  • If you use the email feature: your Gmail address and your app password (the password is encrypted with a key from the Android secure keystore; it is used only to send your emails through Gmail and you can delete it in Settings).
  • If you use backup (Pro): you create the ZIP file and save it wherever you choose; AutoEnvio does not upload it anywhere.

There is no cloud backup: Android does not back up this app's data. If you uninstall the app, all of it is deleted.

7. What each permission is used for

  • SMS (send and receive): send your scheduled SMS and detect incoming ones for the automatic rules.
  • Contacts: choose recipients and show their names. Contacts are not uploaded to any server.
  • Phone calls (make calls): dial the number of a scheduled call.
  • Phone state and call log (only if you turn on an automatic SMS reply to missed or ended calls): know that a call ended and who it was from, in order to reply. The app does not read or store your call history.
  • Notifications: alert you, and read notifications from WhatsApp, WhatsApp Business, Telegram, Messenger and Instagram for auto-replies and forwarding.
  • Accessibility: used only to tap "Send" for you inside WhatsApp, WhatsApp Business, Telegram, Messenger and Instagram, help you pick a chat, and return to AutoEnvio. It is limited to those five apps and only acts while AutoEnvio is sending or choosing a chat; it is not used to read or control the rest of the phone, and AutoEnvio does not collect or send the content of your chats. If you turn on PIN unlock, it also taps the lock-screen keypad, only at the moment of unlocking.
  • Internet: download the app's configuration (the free-version limits) and, if you use the email feature, send your emails through Gmail with your account. AutoEnvio does not send the content of your SMS, WhatsApp, Telegram, Messenger or Instagram messages over the Internet: those apps do that.
  • Exact alarms: send at the exact time you chose.
  • Start on boot: reschedule your sends after the phone restarts or the app is updated.
  • Display over other apps: the countdown before sending, the fake-call screen, and returning to AutoEnvio from another app.
  • Battery (ignore optimization), keep the phone awake, foreground service and vibration: send to many recipients without Android stopping the app, turn the screen on and alert you.
  • Kill background processes: close WhatsApp or Telegram after sending, if you turn that option on.
  • Device administrator: only on Android 7 and 8, to lock the phone after sending, if you turn that option on.

You can remove any permission in Android settings; the features that depend on it will stop working.

8. Retention and security

The app's data is kept on your phone until you delete it or uninstall the app. The PIN and the Gmail app password are stored encrypted with a key from the Android secure keystore. Diagnostic reports you email to us are kept only as long as needed to fix the error.

9. Your rights and how to delete your data

Since almost all of your data is only on your phone, you control it directly: you can view, change or delete sends, history and rules from the app, remove the PIN and the Gmail password in Settings, or uninstall the app to remove everything. To ask us for access, correction or deletion of a diagnostic report you sent us, write to mafero171189@gmail.com.

10. When you send a message

Messages go out through SMS, WhatsApp, WhatsApp Business, Telegram, Messenger or Instagram, or by email (Gmail). You can also schedule calls, fake calls (which do not call anyone) and reminders. From that point, the policies of those services and of your carrier apply. AutoEnvio does not see or store what those services do with the message.

Automatic forwarding sends the content of the messages you receive to another number or email address that you choose. Use it only with your own conversations and inform whoever needs to know.

11. Buying AutoEnvio Pro

AutoEnvio Pro is a one-time payment made through Google Play. Google processes the payment under its own policy; Mi Taller Web does not receive your card details. To recognize your purchase, the app asks Google Play which purchases your Google account has.

12. Children

AutoEnvio is not directed at children under 13.

13. Changes to this policy

If the app changes what it does with your data, this policy is updated and the date above changes.
